Frequently Asked Questions

Is FreshLocal live yet?

FreshLocal is currently being developed and early interest is being collected.

Is FreshLocal a marketplace?

No. FreshLocal is an online directory helping people discover what is available nearby, today.

Who can join?

Smallholders, egg sellers, growers, bakers, food vans, seafood vendors and other local independent sellers.

Will there be an app?

Yes. The FreshLocal app will eventually be available on App Store and Google Play.
